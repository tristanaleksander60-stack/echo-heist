## 2. Target creator profile

**The ideal creator:** posts Roblox videos several times a week; their best videos are friends or siblings playing a new game together and laughing at fails; they return to a game they like (2-3 videos, not one); their thumbnails are honest; their audience is kids and teens who copy what they play.

| Trait | Want | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Platform | YouTube (long + Shorts) and TikTok. Long-form gives longer, smaller spikes; Shorts and TikTok give many short ones (vault) | Twitch-only streamers for this wave (the hour-long CCU spike needs stable servers; later) |
| Size | Micro 10K-200K, mid 200K-2M | Over 2M (macro) until D1 ≥ 22 % |
| Content | **Heist / sneak / escape** games, **duo and co-op funny moments**, **horror-lite** with friends (chase games, "don't get caught"), "new Roblox game" first plays | Drama, "exposing", Robux-giveaway channels, gore or mature games, games with guns as the main hook |
| Cadence | 3+ Roblox posts in the last 14 days | Channels quiet for a month |
| Audience age | **Pre-clearance (before ~Dec 5): audiences that skew 13-17 and older** (MASTERPLAN §9.5 #5), because the game is 16+ only until clearance. After clearance: kid-heavy family channels are welcome | — |
| Conduct | Clean language most of the time, honest thumbnails, no past controversies | "FREE ROBUX", fake rare items, giveaway-for-follows, harassment history |
| Contact | A business e-mail or manager in the bio / About page | Personal DMs to anyone who looks under 18 (contact a parent or manager instead) |
| Language | English first. Optional: 2-3 Danish / Nordic Roblox creators (the owner's home market; native-language pitch) **[DECISION, optional]** | — |

## 4. Safety rules (read before sending anything)

1. **Never DM a creator who may be under 18 from an adult account.** Use the business e-mail or ask for a parent / guardian or manager; keep every message where a parent can see it (vault *Working with Content Creators* §1).
2. **No special powers.** No admin commands, no rigged results, no hidden tools. Creators get what any player could get: a build, a private server, a share link (vault §1).
3. **No off-platform funnels for kids.** We never ask creators to send viewers to a Discord, website or giveaway. The only call to action is "search Heist Yourself on Roblox", and only after clearance.
4. **No bots, no fake traffic,** no "guaranteed players" offers. Decline anyone who offers them (Creator Rewards rules).
5. **Assume anything shown early leaks.** Only show what we'd be fine seeing early. No NDAs (with minors they need a guardian and aren't worth it, vault §5).
6. **We never script praise** or forbid criticism. They say what they really think.

### 5.1 Creator code rules (only if codes are added later, earliest Jan 8)

- A code unlocks **one small free cosmetic, the same for everyone**, once per player. Never Robux, never Cash, never a paid item, never needed to play, never tradeable.

- If the creator earns anything from the code (they don't, unless we later agree a paid deal), they must say **"I get paid when you use my code."** (the wording watchdogs suggested for kids, vault *Short-Form Video Marketing* §5).

## 6. Embargo rules (send these in the acceptance message)

1. **Before the lift: no public posts with gameplay, footage or screenshots** of the Creator Preview or the live game. Private recording is fine and encouraged.
2. **The lift:** Fri Dec 11, 17:00 CET (11:00 US Eastern), when the Christmas update "Wrapping Room" goes live. **Condition:** the game is open to all ages by Wed Dec 9, 17:00 CET. If not, the owner writes to every creator by Wed Dec 9 evening with the new time: **48 hours after the game opens to all ages**.
3. **Why:** until Roblox's check is done, younger viewers can't play yet. We don't want their audiences clicking a game they can't join (MASTERPLAN §9.5 #5).
4. **After the lift:** post whenever you like. Please use the exact title "Heist Yourself: Rob With Your Clones" so search finds it, and your share link where links are allowed (description, bio). No external links are needed inside Roblox.
5. **Tell us your publish time** so the owner can watch the servers (vault §6: a surprise video during a broken update is the worst case). We freeze risky changes 48 h before known videos.
6. If the embargo breaks by accident, no drama: we just ask for the post to be hidden until the lift.

## 7. Disclosure in child-friendly words

Early access is "something of value", so it must be disclosed, even without money (vault *Marketing to Kids and Teens*: Robux, items, **early access** and exclusive cosmetics all count). Send these lines with the acceptance message:

| Situation | Say out loud early in the video, and put on screen | Platform setting |
|---|---|---|
| **Early access only** (our wave) | "The game's developer let me play this early." On screen: `Early access from the developer` | Tick the platform's paid-promotion / branded-content box if its rules count early access **[UNVERIFIED per platform; when unsure, tick it]** |
| **Paid** (only after D1 ≥ 22 %) | "This video is **paid** for by the Heist Yourself developer." On screen: `Paid ad` / `#ad` at the start, not only at the end | Paid-promotion box ticked, always |
| **Code they earn from** (not planned) | "I get **paid** when you use my code." | Paid-promotion box ticked |

Hashtags at the end of a description alone are not enough (vault). Keep a record of what each creator received and the disclosure used (sheet columns `preview_invite_on`, `notes`).

## 11. When it goes wrong (vault §8, short)

| Situation | Do |
|---|---|
| A creator misrepresents the game (fake rewards, "free Robux") | Ask privately for a correction; if refused, end the partnership and state the facts on our own channel |

## 12. Decisions and open questions

**Decisions made here:** no launch date in any message sent before the Nov 30 go ("early December"; the Dec 11 embargo date is always stated with its all-ages condition) (§8); Creator Preview through early-tester invites from Nov 30 (§5); creator clone *trail* instead of a clone colour (§5); no creator codes at launch, neutral code words later (§5.1); early access counts as something to disclose (§7); optional 2-3 Danish / Nordic creators and a 5-creator inner circle (§2, §5).

**Open questions:**
1. Will the creator trail be built (code + art)? If not, drop it from 8.5 and never mention it.
2. Is the game (and its test copies) owned by the owner's account or a group? Early testers must be friends (user-owned) or group members (group-owned), and the limit is 10 per place; a group makes creator invites and future team access easier.
3. Who can be an early tester? Roblox's own Kids & Select page says playtesters who are **Trusted Friends of an age-checked owner** can open the game whatever their age, once the maturity questionnaire is done ([create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/kids-and-select)); third-party guides to the Oct 2026 early-tester feature describe the rule as "similar age or Trusted Friends" **[UNVERIFIED wording, not from Roblox]**. Adult creators added by the adult owner are likely fine; confirm on Nov 16, and see LAUNCH_CHECKLIST (Nov 19) for the kid-wave routes.
4. Which studio account sends friend requests and DMs (a dedicated brand account is safer than the owner's personal one)?
