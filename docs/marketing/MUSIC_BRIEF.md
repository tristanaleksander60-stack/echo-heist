# Music commission brief: "Heist Yourself" (decision D9, approved 2026-11-12)

**What:** two original, stemmed music tracks for a Roblox game, with rights to use them in the game and in our own trailers, TikToks, YouTube Shorts and ads (worldwide, perpetual, including paid ads). Library music can't be used in our own TikToks, which is why we commission.

**Deadline:** delivered by **Mon Dec 7, 2026**. A first sketch (60 s) by Mon Nov 30.

**Budget:** set by the owner from the freelance budget; the composer quotes first, and the owner approves the quote.

## The game in one line

"A Christmas Eve caper in a toy-sized town, and the whole crew is you." You play first person as the Rookie, a cheeky robber in a flat cap; each short heist run is recorded and replayed as copies of your Rookie who help you rob the shop. The guard, Gus, is funny, not scary. The villain, Sir Hoardsworth, has vacuumed up every present in town into his Snow Globe Vault (a 34 s story intro opens the game). The audience is kids 8-13 and their friends, on phones.

## Track 1: main theme, "caper night"

- **Feel:** a sneaky, playful heist caper (pizzicato strings, upright or electric bass, brushed drums, muted trumpet or clarinet, vibraphone). Think toy-box spy film, never dark or violent.
- **Tempo:** about 110-120 BPM, loopable, 90-120 s.
- **The hook:** the **clone arpeggio** is our sound logo. It is C-major pentatonic, one step up per clone, 60 ms apart (MASTERPLAN §7.2). The theme should be built so the arpeggio sits on top in key; ideally the melody quotes it.
- **Stems:** drums, bass, harmony, melody, and a "tension" layer, so the game can add layers as more clones appear and when Gus wakes.

## Track 2: Christmas mix

- The same theme re-orchestrated with sleigh bells, celesta and glockenspiel, plus a light choir "ooh" on the big moment.
- **Same tempo and key**, so the game can cross-fade between the two.

## Stingers (short one-shots, matching key)

- **"8 OF ME!"** choir hit, 2 s.
- **Heist complete,** 3 s.
- **Busted,** 1.5 s, funny, not sad.
- **Sunrise**, the end of the party, 3 s.

## Trailer and intro cut (added 2026-11-13)

- A **30 s trailer edit** of Track 2 (docs/marketing/TRAILER.md): a cold-open hit on frame 1 (the first clone), a darker 8-bar "villain" turn for the intro shots (Hoardsworth, the vacuum, "...GONE."), the caper groove back for the gameplay, the "8 OF ME!" choir hit at 18.6 s, a button ending on the title at 26.4 s. Must work muted (the trailer is captioned) and under platform sounds.
- The intro's 13 cues (docs/AUDIO.md "Intro") can come from the same session if the quote allows: a sting per caption card.

## Delivery

WAV 48 kHz/24-bit stems plus mixed loops. Loudness about -18 LUFS short-term for the in-game loops. Stingers peak at about -6 dBFS.

## Rights checklist

- Original work.
- No samples that carry third-party claims.
- No Content ID registration on the tracks, or a whitelist for our channels, so our own videos are never claimed.
- A written licence naming the uses above.
