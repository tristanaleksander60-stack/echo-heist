# Critique A of PLAN_STEAM_PLUS.md (art and game director lens)

**Verdict: APPROVE WITH CHANGES.** Phase 1 (§1 diagnosis, the clone visibility test, recipe A, the verification ladder) is right and can start now. §3 identity, §5 moments and the §2/§8 gates must be revised before phase 2 starts on Mon Nov 16. ### 2. Give the game a diorama camera and composition rules (C1 is not enough)
"Neon-Noir Diorama" cannot read from a Roblox follow-cam 16 studs behind the player's back. The spawn renders show no table and no cutaway: the player's back hides the objective, and a blown-out white slab fills the bottom fifth of the frame. Replace C1's framing with:
> **Default camera:** pitch 45-55° down, distance 26-32 studs, FOV 40-45 (the long lens is what makes a miniature read), free yaw, pitch clamped to ≥ 35°. A/B against today's camera on the owner's phone on Nov 16.
> **Composition rules (per bookmark, graded):** the player sits in the lower third and the objective in the upper third. The brightest thing in frame is the focal prop. Nothing in the foreground beats the subject for brightness. At least 3 clones are visible in B2 once they exist.

### 3. Rewrite the §3 identity for kids, for Christmas, and for MASTERPLAN §2.2

Level select is a **job board** in the van: pinned photos and cream string. Results is the job sheet with rubber stamps (keep U5). Tape stays as dressing only: the ◀◀ rewind and the counter digits.

Christmas has to read in one frame:

### 7. Make the quality bar ungameable
- **Staged bookmarks.** Four fixed shots per level can be dressed while the rest stays ugly. Add **20 random-time frames** pulled from an unedited R3 playthrough capture of each level, graded the same way.
- **Self-grading.** Claude builds, captures and grades its own shots. Blind reviewers get unlabelled pairs, ours vs a reference frame (Monaco 2, Hitman GO, a top Roblox game). Pass = "comparable" in ≥ 2 of 3 pairings.
- **The luminance floor can be met by flat over-lighting**, which is how Soft Toybox looks. Add caps and ratios: lit faces 1.5-2.5× shadow faces (roblox_quality §0); the focal prop is the brightest non-signal; glow covers 1-16 % of the frame.
- **"≥ 54/60" lets any 6 criteria fail**, including I1 (identity) or C3 (Gus's acting). At launch, groups A, G and I1/I3/I4 must pass in full, plus C3, C6, F1 and H3.
- **The owner's own number:** the owner re-scores POLISH §2's "% finished" table at every gate. Launch requires ≥ 80 % weighted on the owner's score.
- **Kid tests:** add one premium question: a 10 s clip of ours next to 2 top Roblox games, "which looks best?"
- **Fix the phase-1 gate.** Non-negotiable 3 can't pass before U1/U2 land in phase 2. For phase 1, require only "no ▯ boxes, no emoji"; otherwise the gate gets waived.
- **Declare a cut order now.** 53 agent-days in 13-15 days has no slack. Declare what drops first (suggested: dailies to the full bar → L3 polish → jukebox → Gus beats 8-10).
