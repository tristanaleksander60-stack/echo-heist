## 2026-11-12: clone visibility fixed (PLAN_STEAM_PLUS §1 S1, §4.4 A1; clone track)

Templates and rigs with fewer than `CLONE_MIN_JOINTS` whole joints draw as the mannequin (`Shared/Heist/RigJoints`).
